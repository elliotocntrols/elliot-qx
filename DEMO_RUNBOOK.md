# Elliot Qx V8.6 Demo Runbook

## 30-minute live demonstration

1. Sign in and open **Demo Centre**.
2. Click **Create demo project**. Qx creates `QX-DEMO` with sample systems, assets, BMS points, a Category A defect, witness request and turnover package.
3. Select `QX-DEMO` from the project selector.
4. Open **Schedule Importer**.
5. Upload `QX_DEMO_BMS_POINTS.csv`. Confirm Qx detects a BMS points list, review the preview, then click **Generate commissioning pack**.
6. Open **BMS I/O Checkout**. Demonstrate Field / Controller / Graphics / Alarm verification.
7. Open **QA & Commissioning**. Open a generated test sheet and mark a check PASS. Mark one FAIL to demonstrate automatic punch creation.
8. Open **Defects / Punch**. Show Category A as a readiness blocker and update it through rectified / verified / closed.
9. Open **Evidence Hub** and attach a photo or document.
10. Open **Completion Matrix** and **Qx Intelligence** to show exactly what is stopping handover.
11. Repeat Schedule Importer with `QX_DEMO_MSSB_SCHEDULE.csv` to show automatic MSSB asset + board/circuit commissioning sheet generation.
12. Finish in **Reports & Handover / Closeout Studio**.

## Presenter message

Qx is not another digital checklist. A project schedule becomes the structured commissioning workload; the field team records proof once; PMs see blockers live; and the same data becomes handover evidence.
